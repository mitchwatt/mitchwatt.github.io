At Stanford, I taught graduate mathematics preparation, undergraduate market design, and first-year Ph.D. microeconomics.

## Instructor

In 2022, I taught the mathematics camp for incoming economics Ph.D. students and other interested students. The course comprised twenty two-hour sessions covering logic and sets, linear algebra, real analysis, introductory measure theory, convex analysis, static optimization, ordinary differential equations, and optimal control theory.

[Course notes are available here](/files/mathcampnotes22.pdf).

In Summer 2021, I taught the mathematics and economics boot camp for incoming master's students in public policy. The course comprised ten four-hour sessions covering undergraduate calculus, microeconomic analysis, and game theory.

## Teaching Assistant

I received Stanford University's [Centennial Teaching Assistant Award](https://ctl.stanford.edu/centennial-teaching-assistant-awards) for my teaching in undergraduate market design and Ph.D. microeconomics.
